Work

My work puts economics into HR. I built and run Radius Intelligence (RI)'s data department: a workforce analytics platform that reads labour markets not as snapshots of jobs and unemployment but as living systems of skills, transitions, and forward-looking signals, covering every occupation across the US at every depth of geography (MSA, county, state, federal). It runs as three outputs on a shared analytical spine.

  • Economics. Regional economic analysis and labour market forecasting at the core, designed and grounded in macroeconomic theory. The methods produce both explanations and policy levers.

Tech careers workforce development programme

I led the evaluation of sectoral and occupational trends for a major telecommunications company seeking to train 500,000 under-represented individuals for promising tech careers. Through labour market data analysis, skill demand mapping, and infusing job quality as a fundamental criterion, I identified six high-potential occupations now forming the basis of nationwide enrolment and placement across the US. Individuals are currently being enrolled, completing training, and finding employment in these targeted roles.

Job skills and roles forecaster

Active

A new conception of the workforce based on roles and skills rather than occupations, estimating labour-market effects from changing skills and demand of roles rather than specific jobs. The forecaster analyses shifts in skill requirements and role demand, then maps these to workforce needs in terms of occupations, occupational similarity, adjacent positions, and upskilling potential.
