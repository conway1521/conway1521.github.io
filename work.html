Work

My work puts economics into HR. I lead the build of Radius Intelligence, a workforce analytics platform that re-conceives how labour markets are assessed — not as snapshots of jobs and unemployment, but as living systems of skills, transitions, and forward-looking signals — at the level of every occupation, every sector, and every state in the US.

Radius Intelligence

May 2025 – Present · San Francisco
Current

Radius runs two product chains on a single analytical substrate. RI Tech is client-facing: cleaning, standardising, and forecasting employer HR data — turnover, fill rates, vacancy, demand at the SOC level — so health systems and other employers can plan workforce capacity. RI Labs is policy-facing: scoring job opportunity, mapping skill proximity between occupations, and feeding forward-looking market signals into transition pathways for state policymakers, training providers, and economists. Both chains share the same SOC-level core — demand signals, supply data, skill similarity, credential gating, and forecasting — applied at different ends of the labour market.

The Framework

One substrate, two chains

Most labour-market tools see one slice — a sector, a state, a skills taxonomy. Radius is built around a single SOC-level core that spans the whole US labour market, then applies the same underlying logic to two very different surfaces: inside the employer, and across the policy landscape.

RI Labs

Public-Policy Labour-Market Innovation

Macro and meso analytics covering all SOC occupations across all 50 states + DC — scoring opportunity, mapping skill transitions, and reading forward-looking signals.

Plugins

Job Opportunity Index (JOI)

Live

Five-pillar opportunity score for 250+ occupations: job demand, upward mobility, growth trajectory, automation durability, job quality. Goalpost normalisation, volume gating, family-supporting-wage flooring.

Skills Proximity Index (SPI)

Active

O*NET skills, knowledge, and work activities reduced via PCA, projected with UMAP, clustered with HDBSCAN. Produces a cosine-similarity matrix between every occupation pair — the substrate for transition pathways.

Migration Flows

Planned

Net talent flows between states and metros, layered onto the same SOC-level backbone.

Products

State of the State

Live

For each scored occupation, finds reachable destinations via SPI similarity, filters by credential gates, applies wage-premium thresholds, and overlays market signals to flag transitions toward rising or stressed markets.

Job Skills and Roles Forecaster

Active

A new conception of the workforce based on roles and skills rather than occupations, estimating labour-market effects from changing skills and demand of roles rather than specific jobs. The forecaster analyses shifts in skill requirements and role demand, then maps these to workforce needs in terms of occupations, occupational similarity, adjacent positions, and upskilling potential.
