Radius Intelligence (RI)

Head of data science and engineering

May 2025 - Present · San Francisco
Current

I built and run RI's data department: a two-tier labour market intelligence platform that reconciles federal statistics, commercial postings, training and credential records, fiscal signals, and anonymised employer data into one view across every US occupation and geography. RI Technologies handles client-side employer forecasting and workforce planning; RI Labs handles labour market and policy analysis; both run on a compliant multi-tenant data layer of 220 datasets and roughly 8.9M records that grows every time a new client connects.
