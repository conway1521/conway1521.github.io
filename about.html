About

I combine a robust background in economics and public policy with advanced technical expertise in data science and modelling, enriched by significant international exposure from living, studying, and working in Europe and the USA.

I hold a master's degree in Economics from Sciences Po, Europe's leading economics and political science university, where I graduated as a Fulbright Scholar, and a bachelor's degree in Economics and International and Area Studies (double major) from the University of Pittsburgh's Honors College.

Throughout my career, I have applied my technical skills with an intersectional lens to drive forward-thinking, evidence-based, and impactful projects addressing today's most pressing issues. My professional journey is driven by a central aim: to improve quality of life in the global community, especially for those most vulnerable.

I am passionate about applying data-driven insights to foster social impact, climate resilience, and equitable economic growth.
