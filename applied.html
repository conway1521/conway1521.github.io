NED Dashboard: California Regional Analysis

An interactive dashboard exploring regional economic development indicators across California. Built on the theoretical foundations of the OIKOS framework, this tool allows users to examine how place-based conditions, human and social capital, and economic activity interact at the regional level. The dashboard synthesises data from multiple sources to provide a comprehensive view of inclusive development dynamics.
