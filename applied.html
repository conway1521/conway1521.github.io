NED Dashboard: California Regional Analysis

An interactive dashboard exploring regional economic development indicators across California. Developing new theoretical foundations from economic development practitioners' (mayors, community colleges, workforce boards, employers, etc.) field-derived insights, this tool translates those perspectives into quantitative analysis. The dashboard synthesises data from multiple sources to provide a comprehensive and granular view of inclusive development dynamics.
