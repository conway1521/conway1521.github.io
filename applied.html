NED Dashboard: California Regional Analysis

An interactive dashboard exploring regional economic development indicators across California. Developing new theoretical foundations from economic development practitioners' (mayors, community colleges, workforce boards, employers, etc.) field-derived insights, this tool translates those perspectives into quantitative analysis. The dashboard synthesises data from multiple sources to provide a comprehensive and granular view of inclusive development dynamics.

Skills-based Labour Market

Planned

Will allow users to view labour markets as combinations of skill blueprints feeding the roles that then meet demand. Rather than modelling labour as a homogeneous input, we reformulate the production function as:

$$Y = A \cdot R^{\alpha} \cdot K^{\beta}$$

where \( R = f(S) \) is a skills composite and \( S = \left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} \gamma_i \, s_i^{\,\rho}\right)^{1/\rho} \) aggregates individual skills \( s_i \) with elasticity of substitution governed by \( \rho \).

This alternative view of the economy as agglomerations of skills connects with credential-based learning and skills training to show opportunities for upskilling and for adjacent workforces to be tapped to fill gaps, particularly in Manufacturing, to analyse paradigm shifts like the advent of AI on how roles evolve, and to see what skills are more impervious so that workers can build their own resilience. Interactive Jupyter notebook available soon.
