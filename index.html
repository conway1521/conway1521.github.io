I study how labor markets function and fail, with a focus on workforce dynamics and regional economic development. At Radius Intelligence I designed and built the data department end to end: economics, models, pipelines, and product, taking the ecosystem framing from my dissertation into practice.

My PhD develops a framework that treats regional economies as ecosystems, where economic, social, and place-based factors interact in ways traditional models miss. Interests include the future of work, regional economic development, and wellbeing.
