Selected work

Employer digital twin data science · econometrics · product

An employer wants to know: is our turnover bad, what is coming, what should we do about it. The twin takes their raw HR export from upload to forecast and scenarios, every number auditable back to the raw rows.

Labor market digital twin economics · policy

States plan their workforce with statistics that lag by years and projections that arrive as black boxes. The twin models the market as stocks and flows that must balance: who enters, who leaves, demand built four ways, and the gap left over.

Occupation resolver machine learning · LLM systems

Employers name the same job a hundred ways, and nothing downstream works until those titles collapse onto one taxonomy. The resolver fixes this once, for any title, which is why the twins can exist.

Task-based labor market economics · machine learning

The main measures of AI exposure disagree with each other. This is an instrument rather than another estimate: a calibrated production function where every parameter is disclosed, sourced, and swappable, paired with a research paper in progress.

Regional economic development as ecosystem Dissertation · working papers

A framework treating regional economies as integrated ecosystems of place-based conditions, human and social capital, and economic activity, tested on EU regional data. Regions sort into a four-tier ladder, and the pandemic shock moved them down it four times as often as up.
