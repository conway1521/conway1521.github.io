I study how labour markets function and fail, with a focus on workforce dynamics and regional economic development. My work combines macroeconomic modelling, economic geography, and inequality to inform policy. At Radius Intelligence I built and run the data department end to end, taking the ecosystem framing from the dissertation into practice.

My PhD develops a framework that treats regional economies as ecosystems, where economic, social, and place-based factors interact in ways traditional models miss. Interests: the future of work; local and regional economic development; wellbeing.
