Alessandro Conway

Economist working across industry, academic research, and applied tools on labour markets, regional development, and economic ecosystems.

Head of data science and engineeringatRadius Intelligence (RI) PhD candidatein economics,Université Paris 8 MPhilin economics,Sciences Po (Fulbright scholar)

I study how labour markets function and fail, with a focus on workforce dynamics and regional economic development. My research combines macroeconomic modelling, economic geography, and inequality to inform policy decisions. At Radius Intelligence (RI), I built and run the data department end to end, taking the ecosystem framing from the dissertation into practice.

My PhD develops a novel framework conceptualising regional economies as ecosystems where economic, social, and place-based factors interact in ways traditional models miss. This work bridges modern academic pursuits with the experience from field practitioners.

Interests: future of work; local and regional economic development; wellbeing
