LinkedIn GitHub Email Scholar

I study how labour markets function—and fail—with a focus on healthcare workforce dynamics and regional economic development. My research combines economic theory with large-scale data analysis to inform policy decisions. At Radius, I build microsimulation models that help organisations understand and address workforce shortages.

My PhD develops the OIKOS framework, conceptualising regional economies as ecosystems where economic, social, and place-based factors interact in ways traditional models miss. This work bridges my academic training in macroeconomics with practical experience building workforce analytics tools.
