I study how labour markets function and fail, with a focus on workforce dynamics and regional economic development. At Radius Intelligence I designed and built the data department end to end, the economics, the models, the pipelines, and the product, taking the ecosystem framing from my dissertation into practice.

My PhD develops a framework that treats regional economies as ecosystems, where economic, social, and place-based factors interact in ways traditional models miss. Interests: the future of work; local and regional economic development; wellbeing.
