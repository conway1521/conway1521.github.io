Alessandro Conway

Economist working across industry, academic research, and applied tools on labour markets, regional development, and economic ecosystems.

Head of data scienceatRadius Intelligence (RI) PhD candidatein economics,Université Paris 8 MPhilin economics,Sciences Po (Fulbright scholar)

I study how labour markets function and fail, with a focus on workforce dynamics and regional economic development. My research combines macroeconomic modeling, economic geography, and inequality to inform policy decisions. At Radius Intelligence (RI), I lead the build of a workforce analytics platform that puts economics into HR: two product chains running on a shared occupational spine that reconciles fifteen-plus federal, commercial, and academic datasets, one for client-based employer forecasting and workforce planning, and one for public-policy labour-market innovation across all 50 states plus DC.

My PhD develops a novel framework conceptualising regional economies as ecosystems where economic, social, and place-based factors interact in ways traditional models miss. This work bridges modern academic pursuits with the experience from field practitioners.

Interests: future of work; local and regional economic development; wellbeing
