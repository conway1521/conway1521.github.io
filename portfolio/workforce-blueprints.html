Workforce blueprints

How many workers a new facility needs, in what mix, and when, derived when no single source can see it. The worked case: semiconductor fabs, where nine facility types each carry their own recipe and their own ramp.

Economics · data science. The fourth demand layer of the labour market twin.
01

The problem

A chip plant is announced and a region needs to know what it means: how many workers, in which occupations, arriving on what schedule. No source answers this. Announcements give investment and a single headline headcount. Staffing statistics describe yesterday's industry mix, not a leading-edge fab. Filings inform scale and timing but say nothing about composition. The blueprint has to be built, not looked up.

02

How it works

01The recipe, per 100 workers

Industry staffing patterns are decomposed into job families, then re-weighted with type-tuned block totals, an analog fab, a leading-edge logic fab, and a packaging plant are different machines with different recipes. Everything is expressed per 100 workers so it scales to any announcement.

Economics · data science

02Triangulated, with abstentions

Each source covers different axes: staffing matrices see composition but not the frontier. Postings see demand but over-represent churn. Filings see scale and timing but not mix, and abstain from the composition axis by design. Disagreeing estimates reconcile into a band, not a false point.

Economics · data science

03The ramp, not a snapshot

Composition is a trajectory: planned, ramp, steady state, wind-down. Technicians dominate early. Engineering share peaks during tool install. The steady-state mix is what the recipe describes. A blueprint that ignores the ramp misses the years when hiring is hardest.

Economics

04From recipe to region

Blueprint times announced capacity gives forward need by occupation and quarter, the fourth demand layer in the labour market twin, and the one that exists before any requisition is posted.

03

Final state

The blueprint method is built end to end from public sources, staffing matrices, filings, postings, trade announcements, and the recipe shown is a real derivation for a leading-edge logic fab. Nine facility types are identified, each with its own recipe and ramp. The facility scale is illustrative. The shares, and the discipline of abstaining where a source cannot see, are the work.
