Opportunity, and what moves it

Every occupation in a state scored on five visible pillars, and policy levers attached only where the research literature supports them, always with a confidence band, never a bare point.

Policy · economics. Live in one state across 227 scored occupations. Architected for a ten-state cohort.
01

The problem

A workforce board has one real question: which occupations should we back, and what would actually move them. Composite scores usually fail them twice: the score is a black box whose weights nobody can defend, and the interventions arrive without evidence grades, as if a pilot study and a randomised trial were the same thing. Both failures are fixable with discipline.

02

How it works

01Five pillars, kept visible

Demand, wage, access, transition, resilience: each pillar measured from public data and shown next to the score, so the index is never a number you have to take on faith. A wage floor rides along as metadata rather than being buried in the weighting.

Economics · data science

02Stability, tested

Rankings are stress-tested by perturbing the normalisation anchors: if an occupation's rank survives, it is real. If it flips, the index says so. Rank stability holds above 98% under perturbation.

Econometrics

03Levers only with evidence

Interventions come from a curated research corpus, each carrying its effect range, its interval, and an evidence split into causal, quasi-experimental, and observational studies. Null results are retained. Magnitudes are never invented.

Policy · economics

04Synthesis where decisions happen

The index, the levers, and the forward-looking signals compose into standing dashboards on one substrate, per-occupation transition pathways and state-level views a board can actually put in front of its members.

Final state

The index runs in production in one state, 227 occupations fully scored, and is architected to roll out across a ten-state cohort. The lever library holds a curated corpus of about 1,200 research findings mapped through controlled vocabularies, with evidence tiers attached and null results kept. The occupations, pillar values, and lever magnitudes shown here are synthetic. The method is the product.
